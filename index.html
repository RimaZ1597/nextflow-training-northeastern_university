Join us to learn, build, and master Nextflow pipelines!

A free, 3-day hands-on workshop to build reproducible, scalable bioinformatics workflows from scratch

About the Training

Learn Nextflow from beginner to advanced

Develop practical skills using official Nextflow training materials. Explore workflow management and nf-core best practices through guided exercises, with instructor support for questions and troubleshooting

In-person attendance only. No livestream or session recordings will be available

No Installation Needed

All exercises run in GitHub Codespaces, just bring a laptop with a web browser

Experience & Prerequisites

No prior Nextflow experience is required, but some coding experience is helpful

Certificate of Completion

Participants will receive a certificate of completion from Seqera after the training
